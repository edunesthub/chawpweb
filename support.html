Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is delivery?
Most campus orders are delivered within 15-25 minutes depending on the vendor's preparation time.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept Mobile Money (MTN, Telecel, AT) and cash on delivery for selected items.
Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled within 2 minutes of placing them. After the vendor starts preparation, cancellation is not possible.
